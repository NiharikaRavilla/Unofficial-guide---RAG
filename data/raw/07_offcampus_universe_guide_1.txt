Introduction
Choosing the right
apartments near Arizona State University
is one of the most important parts of making your student life comfortable and connected. Whether you want to live steps away from the ASU Tempe campus or prefer a quiet area nearby, there’s an apartment for every lifestyle and budget.
This guide covers the best student housing communities, neighborhoods, and practical tips for living off campus at ASU.
1. Why Live Near ASU?
Living near ASU provides students with flexibility and freedom.
Benefits include:
Walking or biking distance to class.
Access to nearby restaurants, gyms, and entertainment at Mill Avenue.
A large, social student community.
Convenient access to the light rail and campus shuttle.
Tempe’s layout makes it easy to enjoy both academics and leisure.

3. Budgeting for ASU Housing
Tempe offers various rent options for students at different price points.
Average monthly rent:
Shared room:
$900–$1,200
Studio:
$1,400–$1,800
One-bedroom:
$1,800–$2,400
Utilities & Wi-Fi:
$100–$160
Budget tips:
Share an apartment to reduce costs.
Cook at home instead of eating out frequently.
Use public transportation to avoid parking fees.
4. Roommates, Leases, and Rental Tips
Before committing to a lease:
Review all rental terms for deposits and maintenance policies.
Ask if rent includes Wi-Fi or utilities.
Meet your roommates to ensure compatibility.
Keep a copy of your lease and digital records of all payments.
5. Safety and Neighborhood Security
Tempe is generally student-friendly, but smart habits are essential:
Choose apartments with gated entry and 24/7 management.
Register for
ASU Police alerts
for campus safety updates.
Avoid walking alone late at night.
Use the
ASU Safety Escort Service
when needed.

Commuting to and around ASU is simple:

Free neighborhood routes for ASU students.

Safe and common for students.

7. Touring Apartments and Avoiding Scams
To rent safely:
Tour apartments before paying deposits.
Avoid landlords who ask for large payments upfront.
Compare prices across listings to identify scams.
Use verified student housing platforms or ASU’s official listings.
