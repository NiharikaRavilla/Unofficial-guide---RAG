Transportation in Tempe

Tempe public transportation is served by the Valley Metro Rail system, connecting the city to neighboring communities, such as Phoenix and Mesa. Here are some popular mode of transportation among the citizens in Tempe AZ US:
1.
Valley Metro Rail
:
The light rail line runs for 30 miles and boasts 41 stations with 9 stops in Tempe. The Valley Metro Rail runs 7 days a week, both on working days and weekends, with 18-22 hours per day and 15-20 minutes waiting times between trains. The price of a one-day pass costs $4. Valley Metro Rail connects Tempe to some popular destinations like the Phoenix Art Museum, Heard Museum, and the Mill Avenue district in downtown Tempe. In addition, via the light rail, passengers can also connect to the PHX Sky Train at the 44th Street/Washington station to access the Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport.
2.
Valley Metro Buses
: The bus network operated by Valley Metro is also well-developed in Tempe. Buses run throughout Tempe, also connecting to other cities in the Phoenix metro area. Moreover, the NextRide system is available for you to get real-time bus arrival information by phone or text. There is also the door-to-door transportation provided by Valley Metro Dial-a-Ride service for those with disabilities, which works 7 days per week.
3.
Ride-share
: If you are looking for a more private way of transportation, ride-sharing services like Uber or Lyft are the suitable choices throughout Tempe.
4.
Electric Scooters
: The E-scooter is another popular transportation options for the Tempe citizens when having the short trip around downtown Tempe and other parts of the city.
Overall, Tempe boasts a robust public transit system centered around the Valley Metro light rail and bus network, bringing residents and visitors with convenient, affordable options for getting around Tempe.
