User: funkymonkey7777

I am a graduate student at ASU and have lived in multiple apartments around Tempe.

Places to avoid:

West 6

* Huge party atmosphere
* Very noisy
* Poor management

Apollo

* Similar environment to West 6
* Things constantly breaking

Oliv

* Friends reported bad experiences

Rise

* Friends reported excessive noise

University House

Places I recommend:

Redpoint

* Good experience
* Does not have 1x1 apartments

Park Place

* Great place overall
* Noise depends on neighbors and pool-facing units

The Local Apartments
The Emerson

* Great places
* More expensive

Union Tempe

* Recommend North side units
* South side less desirable
* Amenities occasionally closed
