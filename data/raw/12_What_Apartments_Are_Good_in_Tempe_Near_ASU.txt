TITLE: What Apartments Are Good in Tempe, Near ASU?

SOURCE: Reddit r/ASU

POST

Budget:

* Around $1,700/month

Preferences:

* Close to ASU
* Near Mill Avenue and campus activity

User: poopiepickle

Oliv and Union are decent options.

I lived in:

* Union (4 bed / 2 bath)
* Oliv (Studio)

I enjoyed both.

Both have:

* Good amenities
* Nice gyms
* Excellent walkability to Mill Avenue and campus

If I had to choose one, I would pick Oliv.

Union's biggest issue is elevators.

There is almost always at least one elevator out of service.

My unit at Union had a very large balcony, floor-to-ceiling windows, and a great view.

Parking is expensive at both properties, around $160/month.

I parked at West 6 instead and walked to my car.
