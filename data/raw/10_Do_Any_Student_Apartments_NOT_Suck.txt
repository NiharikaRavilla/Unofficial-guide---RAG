TITLE: Do Any Student Apartments NOT Suck?

SOURCE: Reddit r/ASU

POST

* Puking students
* Bad smells

* Poor maintenance
* Homeless people wandering around
* Broken gates and locks
* Theft

User: dazedconfusedev

Apartment reviews are not always reliable.

Unhappy residents tend to leave reviews while happy residents usually move on with their lives.

I've read reviews of places I lived and sometimes found them difficult to recognize based on my own experience.

User: Sylvie_Loki_2021

I lived at District on Apache for two years and generally enjoyed it.

The location close to campus increases the price significantly.

Management was poor.

The biggest issue for me was roommates rather than the apartment itself.

If possible, finding a good roommate or living alone is ideal.

Check Facebook housing groups for subleases and ask people why they are moving out before taking over a lease.

* 922 Place
* District
* Union
